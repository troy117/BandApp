# Song library and student access

Open **Song library** to search titles/file names, filter by part and sort by title, update date or length. Teachers can also filter by student access. Pick an instrument beside **Practice**; a student’s default part is selected when the song contains it. The desktop list scrolls with fixed column headings; phones use stacked rows.

**Manage** contains Audio & backing, Edit parts & lyrics and Archive. Archived songs keep their files and can be restored from **Archived songs**. Device songs can be published for students or teachers only.

## Student access

For a song you manage, choose its **Student access**:

- **Students & teachers**: available in student school libraries, instrument song selectors and jams.
- **Teachers only**: remains available to teachers for testing and setup. Students cannot list or request the song, its arrangements or any revision of its MIDI/backing files.

The song’s publishing teacher and the school owner can change access. All teachers can view teacher-only songs; other teachers cannot edit another teacher’s songs. Students have no access-setting controls.

End any current jam using a song before switching it to Teachers only. Teacher-only songs are omitted from jam song choices; server checks also prevent assigning one through a stale screen. Existing saved practice reports stay retained.

Both import/publishing workspaces offer a visibility choice for a new school song. Later edits keep the song’s current access; change it in Song library. Archive/restore keeps the same access setting. Existing school songs default to their previous shared behavior. Access flags are included in normal school database backups, alongside the unchanged songs and accounts.
