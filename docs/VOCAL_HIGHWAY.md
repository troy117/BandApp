# Vocal highway and longer warm-up — Prototype 17

Under Vocals, choose **Pitch highway** or **Large lyrics**. The highway is the default for parts with a saved melody; lyric-only parts retain the large text view. This choice is a browser display preference and can change during playback without restarting the song. Pause, continue, seek, retry-line, entrance preview, focus view and teacher-led jam playback retain their existing transport.

Melody bars scroll right to left toward a white sing line. Higher bars represent higher pitches and longer bars represent longer holds. Supplied MIDI pitches/onsets/releases use the existing song clock. Whole-octave guide choices shift the displayed reference. With microphone access enabled, a small local voice trace shows measured pitch near the play line: green near target, pink above/below. Comfortable-octave mode projects the measured pitch into the displayed melody's octave; the Target/You card retains the actual detected note names. No trace is invented for silence, rests, count-in, ambiguous targets or microphone-off playback. Trace history clears on a seek, part change or octave change and is never saved or uploaded.

Lyrics below the bars use the saved line start/end times and text-length weighting, matching the existing white-to-yellow karaoke sweep. Word placement is approximate; no new syllable alignment is inferred. The current and upcoming lines remain readable underneath, including the existing two-bar entrance preview after long instrumental breaks. Imported lyric text is rendered as text, never executed as HTML.

In **Sound & pitch settings → Warm up your voice**, each reference note now sounds for four seconds at a sustained level. A 0.3-second quiet gap precedes a four-second response. Listen/Your turn countdowns identify the phase. The dedicated warm-up meter shows Target, You and a low/center/high indicator during the response only. It stays neutral during the reference/gap, uncertain input and microphone-off practice. Existing pitch tolerance and 0.7-second held-note matching remain. The five-note opening exercise takes about 42 seconds.

The comfortable-octave finder retains its steady-note suggestion and Apply action. Closing settings, cancelling, starting a song, hiding/leaving the page or changing parts/accounts stops the warm-up voice. This is optional, local practice feedback, with no vocal grade, recording, school-data revision, schema or Railway variable changes. Preserve the existing persistent volume and school music.

Automated checks cover reference/release timing, neutral/low/high meter states, lyric bounds, held-note geometry, octave shifts, finite phone/fullscreen rendering, trace sampling and seek resets. Browser QA uses generated audio without accessing the physical microphone. Real phone singing and comfort testing remain useful.
